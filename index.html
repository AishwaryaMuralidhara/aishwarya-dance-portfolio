About the Artist

"

Aishwarya Muralidhara is a Bharatanatyam artist of rare depth and grace — a dancer whose body becomes the living language of an ancient tradition. With over a decade of devoted practice, she trained under Vid. Smt. Vani Venkata Ramu at Nirmala Natya Kalanikethana and under Vid. Smt. Ranjini Umesh at Raagini Sangeetha Nruthyalaya — two luminous lineages flowing through her every movement.

A Computer Science Engineer by training, she brings to her art the same precision she applies to logic. A practitioner of the Veena, she deepens her musicality from within. As an accomplished competitive swimmer, she brings the athlete's discipline — a body that sustains and a mind that remains fully present. In her, dancer, engineer, musician, and athlete speak the same language: Bharatanatyam in its fullest splendour.

Bharatanatyam Veena Computer Science Engineer Competitive Swimmer
